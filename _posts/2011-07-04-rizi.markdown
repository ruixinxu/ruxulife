<div class="lang-zh" markdown="1">

用抽屉锁住自己的秘密

在喜爱的书上留下批语

信投进信箱 默默地站一会儿

风中打量着行人 毫无顾忌

留意着霓虹灯闪烁的橱窗

电话间里投进一枚硬币

向桥下钓鱼的老头要支香烟

河上的轮船拉响了空旷的汽笛

在剧场门口幽暗的穿衣镜前

透过烟雾凝视着自己

当窗帘隔绝了星海的喧嚣

灯下翻开褪色的照片和字迹

</div>

Lock your secrets away in a drawer

Leave notes in the margins of books you love

Drop a letter into the mailbox, then stand there silently for a while

Study the passersby in the wind, without any restraint

Notice the neon-lit shop windows flickering

Slip a coin into a phone booth

Ask the old man fishing under the bridge for a cigarette

The ship on the river sounds its hollow whistle

Before the dim dressing mirror at the theater entrance

Gaze at yourself through the smoke

When the curtains shut out the noise of the sea of stars

Under the lamp, open the faded photographs and handwriting
